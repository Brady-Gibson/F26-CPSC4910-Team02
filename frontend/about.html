Good Driver Incentive Program

Sign inDrivers · Sponsors · Admins

Rewarding truck drivers for safe, on-time driving.

How it works

Trucking companies sponsor their drivers and reward good driving with points that turn into real products.

  1. Drive safeSponsors track on-time deliveries, clean inspections, and safe driving habits.
  2. Earn pointsPoints are added or removed with a clear reason, so drivers always know why their balance changed.
  3. Redeem rewardsDrivers spend points in a catalog their sponsor builds from a live product feed.

Who it's for

One platform, three kinds of accounts.

DriversApply to a sponsor, track points, and redeem rewards.
SponsorsReview applications, award points, and manage their catalog.
AdministratorsManage accounts and sponsors, and review activity logs.
