How it works

Trucking companies sponsor their drivers and reward safe, on-time driving with points. Sponsors can add or remove points at any time and always give a reason, so drivers know exactly why their balance changed.

Drivers spend their points in a catalog their sponsor builds from a live product feed. Orders deduct points right away, and cancelled orders are refunded.

Who uses it

  • DriversApply to a sponsor, track points, and redeem rewards.
  • SponsorsReview applications, award points, and manage the catalog.
  • AdminsManage accounts and sponsors, and review audit logs.
